title: "A $3000 textbook on reinforcement learning"
---

Yesterday I was reading a soft copy (pdf version) of Prof. Dimitri Bertsekas's book, *A Course in Reinforcement Learning*, and at some point thought that I may want to get a hard copy of this book. So naturally I went to Amazon and searched for it. Then I could not believe my eyes: the book is priced at \\$3000 (\\$2999.99 + \\$3.99 shipping fee to be exact). And it's a "Used: Acceptable" book, not even a brand new one!

Nevertheless, I'll stick to the soft copy for now :).
